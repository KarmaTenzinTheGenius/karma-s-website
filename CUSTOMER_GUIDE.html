📚 How to Use Karma Lokdruel Tshongley Store

A complete guide for customers to shop and enjoy our e-commerce store

🏠 Getting Started

Welcome to our online store! Here's how to get started:

1 Visit the Store

Open index.html in your web browser

2 Browse Products

You'll see a grid of products with images, names, prices, and star ratings

3 Explore Features

Use search, categories, and cart features explained below

✨ Main Features

📁 Category Filtering

Look at the category thumbnails at the top of the product section:

How to use:
  • Click on a category thumbnail (e.g., 📱 Electronics, 👕 Clothing)
  • The category will highlight in blue
  • Only products from that category will be shown
  • Click "All" (🏪) to see all products again

🔍 Product Search

Use the search bar in the top navigation:

How to use:
  • Type a product name, brand, or description
  • Click the "Search" button or press Enter
  • You'll be taken to a search results page
  • Only products matching your search will be shown
  • Click "Back to Home" to return to the main store

🛒 Shopping Cart

Add items to your cart and checkout:

How to use:
  • Find a product you want to buy
  • Click "Add to Cart" button
  • The cart counter in the top-right will increase
  • Click the cart button (🛒 Cart) to view your cart
  • See your items, quantities, and total price
  • Click "Proceed to Checkout" to buy or "Clear Cart" to remove all items

⭐ Product Reviews

Read reviews from other customers and write your own:

How to use:
  • Click "📝 Reviews" button on any product
  • See all existing reviews and ratings
  • To write a review, fill out the form:
  • - Your name (required)
    - Select a star rating (1-5 stars)
    - Write a review title
    - Write your comment (optional)
  • Click "Submit Review"
  • Your review will be visible to other customers

🔥 Hot Discounts

See products currently on sale:

How to use:
  • Look for the "Hot Discounts" section at the top
  • Shows products with special discounts
  • See original price and discounted price
  • Click "Add to Cart" to buy a discounted item

📦 Understanding Product Cards

Each product is shown as a card with the following information:

  • Product Image: Visual representation of the item
  • Product Name: The name of the product
  • Category: What type of product (Electronics, Clothing, etc.)
  • Price:
    • Original price (crossed out if on sale)
    • Final price (what you actually pay)
    • Discount percentage badge if applicable
  • Star Rating: Average rating from customer reviews (e.g., ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.5)
  • Review Count: How many customers reviewed this product
  • Add to Cart Button: Click to add item to shopping cart
  • Reviews Button: Click to read and write reviews

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I search for a product?

A: Type in the search bar at the top and click "Search" or press Enter. You'll be taken to a results page showing matching products.

Q: Can I filter products by category?

A: Yes! Click on a category thumbnail (like 📱 Electronics) to see only products from that category. Click "All" to see all products.

Q: How do I add items to my cart?

A: Click the "Add to Cart" button on any product. The cart counter at the top will increase.

Q: Will my cart be saved if I close the browser?

A: Yes! Your cart is automatically saved in your browser. When you come back, your items will still be there.

Q: How do I see my shopping cart?

A: Click the cart button (🛒 Cart) in the top-right corner of the page. A popup will show all your items and total price.

Q: Can I write a review for a product?

A: Yes! Click the "📝 Reviews" button on any product to see reviews or write your own. You can rate with 1-5 stars and write comments.

Q: What do the star ratings mean?

A: ⭐ = 1 star (poor), ⭐⭐ = 2 stars (fair), ⭐⭐⭐ = 3 stars (average), ⭐⭐⭐⭐ = 4 stars (good), ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ = 5 stars (excellent)

Q: How do I find discounted products?

A: Look at the "Hot Discounts" section at the top of the products, or look for products with a discount badge showing the percentage off.

Q: What's the difference between original price and final price?

A: If a product is on sale, the original price is shown with a strikethrough. The final price (in bold) is what you actually pay.

🤝 Need Help?

If you encounter any issues:

  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection
  • Try refreshing the page (press F5 or Ctrl+R)
  • Clear your browser cache if things look broken
  • Try using a different browser if problems persist
  • Check that JavaScript is enabled in your browser
